UNIVERSITY PRINTING SERVICE

Students can print from university computer rooms and supported personal devices.

Basic procedure:
1. Connect to the university network.
2. Select a university printer.
3. Submit the document to the print queue.
4. Authenticate at the printer.

Common problems include an offline printer, insufficient print credit, a stuck print queue, or lack of network connectivity.

For Wi-Fi authentication problems, consult the Wi-Fi support documentation rather than changing printer settings.
