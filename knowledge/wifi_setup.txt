UNIVERSITY WI-FI SETUP AND TROUBLESHOOTING

Network name: eduroam

Windows
1. Open Settings > Network & Internet > Wi-Fi.
2. Select eduroam and enter your current university username and password.
3. If you recently changed your university password, Windows may still have the old password cached.
4. Choose "Forget" for the existing eduroam network, then reconnect using your new password.
5. If connection still fails, confirm that your university account is active.

macOS
Open System Settings > Wi-Fi, select eduroam, and authenticate with your current university credentials. If old credentials are stored, remove the existing eduroam configuration and reconnect.

Android and iOS
Select eduroam in Wi-Fi settings and authenticate using current university credentials.

General troubleshooting
- Check that Wi-Fi is enabled.
- Verify that the username and password are current.
- Check the university service status before assuming the problem is device-specific.
